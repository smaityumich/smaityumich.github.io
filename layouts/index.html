About

Statistical foundations for learning in changing environments

My current work focuses on transfer learning and learning under distribution shift—how to borrow information across related datasets without losing reliability in the target population.

I received my PhD in Statistics from the University of Michigan, advised by Yuekai Sun and Moulinath Banerjee. Before that, I studied mathematics and statistics at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.
